# Embervale — Asset Manifest

## Visual Target

**Art direction:** An isometric storybook forest RPG diorama at blue-hour dusk. The miniature forest uses bottle-green ground, lichen stone, cedar bark, and fogged blue-grey shadows. A player lantern and restoration beacon are the strongest warm amber lights; the Hushling is a restrained cool-teal counterpoint. The world is a tactile tabletop folktale, not pixel art or generic high fantasy.
