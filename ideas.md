# Embervale: The Lantern Path — Design Exploration

## Three Directions Considered

### 1. Moss & Candlewax
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, hand-painted tabletop fantasy where lamplight, wax seals, and mossy stone make a short journey feel intimate and storybook-like. The atmosphere favors discovery over spectacle.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Runeprint Chronicle
**Very Brief Intro:** A graphic-novel RPG rendered as inked parchment, with elaborate runic diagrams and sharply limited mineral pigments. It feels archival, mysterious, and slightly austere.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Moonsteel Arcade
**Very Brief Intro:** A fast neon-dark action RPG with chrome UI, spectral enemies, and arcade momentum. It prioritizes immediacy and high contrast.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction: Moss & Candlewax

### Design Movement
**Cozy gothic storybook illustration** translated into an isometric miniature diorama. It takes cues from illustrated folktales, hand-painted tabletop terrain, and quiet woodland fantasy—not generic pixel art or high-fantasy spectacle.

### Core Principles
1. **A tiny world with tactile depth:** Layered ground, raised ruins, lanterns, and deliberately placed vegetation should feel like a crafted game board.
2. **Legible adventure at a glance:** The hero, enemy, objective, and interaction prompt receive focused color and lighting contrast.
3. **Soft menace, never grimness:** The woodland holds mild corruption and shadow, but amber light and botanical greens make it inviting.
4. **A compact but complete loop:** Explore, meet a foe, make a combat choice, grow stronger, and restore a local landmark in one sitting.

### Color Philosophy
The world is built from a dark bottle-green ground, lichen greens, cedar brown, and fogged blue-grey stone. **Lantern Amber (#F6B84B)** is the signature color: it represents courage, the quest goal, selected actions, and safe points. This light should always read as a purposeful warm counterpoint to the cool forest.

### Layout Paradigm
The game is an **isometric quest tableau** framed by a thin, engraved HUD rather than a conventional page layout. The wide world occupies the visual center; a vertical quest ledger and compact action tray hang from the frame’s edges as if pinned to a tabletop map.

### Signature Elements
1. A pooled, animated amber glow around lanterns and the player’s carried light.
2. An engraved brass-and-parchment HUD with stained-glass health gems and a wax-seal quest marker.
3. Curving pale paths through the dark grove, ending at a visibly repaired beacon altar.

### Interaction Philosophy
Movement is direct and physical: WASD or arrow keys move the lantern-bearer, while clear button controls support touch. Combat pauses the encounter, showing only meaningful choices—**Strike**, **Guard**, and **Mend**—with immediate visible consequences rather than dense menus.

### Animation
The world should breathe subtly: lanterns pulse, mist drifts, leaves sway, and the player’s beacon has a warm rhythmic aura. Movement eases in and out; hit reactions are short, readable camera nudges. Interface feedback uses restrained scale and opacity transitions below 220ms. Reduced-motion preferences stop environmental drift and use static indicators.

### Typography System
**Cinzel Decorative** serves as the carved-folio display face for the title, quest title, and encounter names. **DM Sans** supports numerical HUD data and readable instructions. Display type uses sparse uppercase tracking; body copy stays sentence case with generous line-height.

### Brand Essence
**A pocket-sized adventure for players who want a complete forest quest in a few focused minutes, distinguished by a living lantern-light atmosphere.**

Personality: **handcrafted, hopeful, quietly mysterious.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines read like passages from a field journal; CTAs are plain-spoken adventure verbs, never generic software language.

> “Carry the flame where the grove has gone quiet.”

> “Light the beacon.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The logo is a **cut-paper lantern inside a circular woodland knot**, with a small opening suggesting a path into the grove. The wordmark uses custom-spaced, engraved letterforms rather than a default-font treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
**Lantern Amber — #F6B84B.**

## Style Decisions

- The opening view must always make the miniature grove, a Lantern Amber light source, and an engraved brass-and-parchment HUD frame visible together.
- Lantern Amber is reserved for quest purpose, safety, selected actions, the player’s carried flame, and the beacon; it is never a generic decorative yellow.
- Microcopy follows a concise field-journal voice: **Carry**, **Guard**, **Mend**, and **Light** are preferred action verbs, while generic software phrasing is excluded.
